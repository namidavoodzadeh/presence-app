# Package 01: the rules it must stay inside

The product laws can change; these lines cannot. Each row says where package 01 meets the line, or what is still missing. This is a product check, not legal advice: a lawyer should confirm the open rows before the App Store submission.

| Line | Where it comes from | How package 01 meets it | Still missing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ask before sending personal data to a third-party AI, and name it** | App Store guideline 5.1.2(i), as revised 13 Nov 2025: clearly disclose where personal data is shared with third parties, including third-party AI, and get explicit permission first | `aiConsent` before the first model call names Anthropic, says what is sent and what is not; Settings turns it off; with it off nothing is sent (`flows/privacy.md`) | The server side (`GET /me ai_consent`, `POST`/`DELETE /consent/ai`, the model-call gate) is a proposal (CONTRACT §11.4). The privacy page at `site/privacy` does not name Anthropic yet; Apple asks the privacy policy to name the third parties and the data |
| **Account deletion inside the app** | App Store guideline 5.1.1(v); with Sign in with Apple, revoke the token through Apple's REST API | Settings → Delete my account, one confirming card, `DELETE /account`, which revokes the Apple token first | — |
| **A privacy-focused sign-in beside Google** | App Store guideline 4.8 | Sign in with Apple is offered beside Google, the same size | — |
| **Adults only, and the store's age signal** | Thesis (adults only); Texas SB 2420 in effect since June 2026, with Utah and Louisiana laws in 2026 | Declared Age Range with a gate at 18 before sign-in; under 18 cannot sign in; the 18+ statement where the range is not available | The App Store age rating set to 18+ in App Store Connect (owner) |
| **Not therapy** | Thesis "Not therapy"; Illinois (Aug 2025), Nevada and Utah laws on AI mental-health services | No diagnosis, treatment, advice or mental-state labels on any screen (law 7, `copy.md`); Talk asks about how a person communicates, not how they feel inside (law 1) | The App Store description and site copy follow the claims rule (CGO) |
| **Say it is an AI, and route crisis to people** | California SB 243 (in force 1 Jan 2026) and Utah, for chatbots that could be taken for a person; thesis law 8 | "Kenember is an AI, not a person." on sign-in, on the consent screen and in Settings; the app never says "I" and has no face; any risk turn stops at once and shows crisis resources (law 8) | Whether SB 243's "companion chatbot" definition reaches Kenember is a legal question; if it does, it also asks for a published safety protocol and yearly reports from July 2027 |
| **Special-category data** | Law 10; GDPR article 9 if EU users are invited | No training on user content; Download your data and Delete my account in one tap each; What lands hard and This week are not offered in package 01 because their field-level encryption is not built (CPO spec) | Encryption for those two kinds, before they are offered |
| **Speech stays on the phone** | Law 10; Apple's speech-recognition permission rules | Recognition runs with `requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true`; where it isn't supported the mic is hidden; the permission text says words are turned into text on the iPhone | — |
| **Notifications carry nothing private** | V3 rule 12; law 10 | The push says "A new card is ready." with no name and no words (CONTRACT §12.4) | — |
| **Nothing reaches another person unseen** | V3 rule 7; laws 3 and 10 | The gift on an invite and every card are shown exactly as the other person will see them before they go; no grant is written before the invite is accepted | The invite-gift route (`routes.md`) |
| **No raw outside input** | Laws 11 and 12 | Mirror is not in package 01 | — |
